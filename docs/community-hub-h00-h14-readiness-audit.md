# H00–H14 acceptance audit — 11 September 2026

This is an evidence map, not a declaration that the complete Hub is ready to
release. It follows the exit gates in
[the implementation plan](community-hub-implementation.md#13-ordered-work-packages).
The [execution ledger](community-hub-execution.md) remains authoritative for
chronological results and subsequent corrections. H15 and final release artifact
qualification belong to the [separate release report](community-hub-release-readiness.md). Production deployment and
publication are not authorized.

Paths beginning `master/`, `base/`, `app/` and `launcher/` below refer to the
dedicated `hub-master`, `hub-base`, `hub-app` and `hub-launcher` worktrees. Evidence
under `artifacts/` is local and sanitized; private fixture credentials must not be
included in a release evidence bundle.

  launcher UI against fixture transports. They do not establish the Next website
  journey, native browser-shell execution, or Cyberpunk asset behavior.
- The real local master/files/worker chain has accepted packages, performed
  moderation, and delivered exact bytes. These are API/script journeys, not
  creator A → reviewer → user B interactions through the website.
- Base `37875ab1` records an actual separate-process install/restart/update/
  restart/rollback/restart sequence. Its inert archive proves classification and
  transaction behavior, not real game asset mounting. The unrelated Cyberware
  clients were preserved; latest recorded VRAM was 10,864/12,227 MiB.

## Release implication

The implementation is substantially integrated and has meaningful local API,
filesystem, database, scanner and separate-process evidence. It is still not
appropriate to check off every H00–H14 gate. The remaining cross-cutting blockers
are the rejected Next startup, missing real GitHub-provider credentials/acceptance,
and genuine game-client/preload validation. The versioned schema/licensed sample
handoff artifacts and owner review of public policy copy also remain required.
Final schema38 regression and H15
artifact/operational qualification remain separate active work.

This audit made no service changes, launched no website or game, and performed no
production deployment or publication.
