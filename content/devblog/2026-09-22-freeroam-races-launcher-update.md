Today’s work focused on making moment-to-moment play feel better in OPEN//77: smoother Freeroam activities, cleaner race behavior, and a more readable launcher. For anyone following this Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer mod, the update is less about flashy backend work and more about reducing friction where players actually feel it.

## Freeroam adds a new Blade FFA and HUD improvements

Freeroam got a meaningful content and presentation pass. The biggest gameplay addition is **Blade FFA**, a new activity that gives players another fast, direct way to jump into combat during the stress test. More quick-start activities matter in a multiplayer sandbox because they shorten the time between spawning in and actually doing something fun.

The team also added an **Overdrive HUD** update for Freeroam. HUD work is easy to underestimate, but in a Cyberpunk 2077 online session it does a lot of heavy lifting: it helps players understand what mode they are in, what state they are in, and what the game expects next. Better visual feedback means less confusion and a cleaner first impression for new testers.

## Foot races should behave more reliably now

A good chunk of today’s progress went into race flow, especially for players on foot. OPEN//77 now includes **foot race recovery improvements**, which should make it easier to get back into the activity when something goes wrong during setup or transition.

Two specific race issues were also fixed.

First, **foot runners now stay frozen throughout grid synchronization**. That matters because multiplayer race starts depend on every player seeing a fair, consistent setup. If a player can move too early or gets released at the wrong time, the whole start feels broken. Keeping runners properly locked until synchronization finishes makes starts more predictable and more competitive.

Second, **double jump is restored correctly after race grid transitions**. Movement in Night City is a huge part of the feel of the game, so losing mobility after switching in and out of races is the kind of bug players notice immediately. Restoring that cyberware behavior keeps traversal feeling like Cyberpunk instead of a restricted minigame.

## Launcher readability gets a quality-of-life pass

The launcher also received a practical update. The **server workspace is now more compact**, which should help players browse information with less clutter and help future server owners present their server listings more cleanly.

On top of that, **in-game identity names are shown more clearly**. This is a small feature on paper, but it has a real impact in a Cyberpunk 2077 co-op or RP context. Clear identity display helps players understand who they are signed in as, and it makes the transition from account-level UI to character-level play feel more coherent.

For a project building toward a full Cyberpunk 2077 dedicated server ecosystem, these details matter. Good multiplayer UX is not only about netcode and performance; it is also about making names, sessions, and servers easy to understand at a glance.

## Stress test sessions are easier to identify

The in-game experience now includes a **compact Open Stress Test session signature**. This is mainly a presentation and identification improvement, but it is useful during active testing, screenshots, event organization, and general player support. When a community stress test is happening, being able to quickly recognize the session context helps everyone stay on the same page.

## Why this update matters

This was a polish-heavy day, but it was the kind of polish that directly improves how OPEN//77 feels to play. New Freeroam content gives testers more to do, race fixes remove frustrating edge cases, and launcher cleanup makes the whole experience easier to read.

For players waiting on broader Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer progress, this is a good sign: the project is not just adding features, it is tightening the loop around the features people already touch. For future Cyberpunk 2077 RP server operators and event hosts, the launcher and session-identity work also points toward a cleaner platform experience as server discovery and player onboarding continue to improve.

More to come from Night City online soon. 🚦
