OPEN//77 got a quality-of-life update focused on the first few minutes players spend with the platform: creating an account, signing in, and finding a server worth joining. Today’s work also adds Discord linking in the launcher and fixes a stubborn freeze on Cyberpunk 2077 2.5.

## Faster account access for Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer

The biggest player-facing change today is a smoother account flow. When automatic verification is active, new users can now sign in immediately after registering instead of getting stuck waiting on an e-mail step.

That matters more than it sounds. For a **Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer mod**, the worst first impression is friction before the game even starts. If an e-mail provider is delayed or having a bad day, players should not feel like the whole platform is down. This change keeps onboarding moving and reduces the chance that a new player bounces before ever reaching the launcher.

The team also added temporary support for automatic e-mail verification during provider outages. This is not a flashy feature, but it is exactly the kind of platform resilience that helps a live service feel dependable. The goal is simple: if Night City is online, account creation should feel online too.

## Discord linking arrives in the launcher

The launcher can now connect and manage linked Discord accounts through OAuth. Once linked, the launcher also shows the Discord avatar in the header.

For players, this is a convenience feature with a nice community touch. OPEN//77 accounts now feel more connected to the spaces where communities actually organize, recruit, and chat. For future features, account linking creates a cleaner foundation for server communities, identity, and social tooling without adding more passwords or profile setup steps.

The avatar display is a small change, but it makes the launcher feel more personal. When players open the launcher for **Cyberpunk 2077 online**, they now get a stronger sense that the platform is becoming a full ecosystem rather than just a patcher and login box.

## Smarter server discovery in Night City online

The server directory now defaults to the most populated servers.

That means new and returning players are more likely to see active places first instead of a random list. For a **Cyberpunk 2077 RP server** or any community trying to build momentum, discoverability matters. Showing busy servers up front helps players find action faster, and it helps healthy servers convert curiosity into actual joins.

This is one of those changes that sounds obvious in hindsight. In practice, it can make the difference between a quiet first impression and immediately spotting where people are already playing.

## Stability fix for Cyberpunk 2077 2.5

Today also includes a fix for an appearance-restore freeze affecting Cyberpunk 2077 2.5 in certain situations.

This was the kind of issue that does not show up as a headline feature, but absolutely affects confidence in a **Cyberpunk 2077 co-op** experience. Freezes and lockups break immersion fast, especially when they are hard to predict. Squashing bugs like this is part of the long, unglamorous work that turns a prototype into something players can trust for longer sessions.

For server owners and regular players alike, stability work is one of the most valuable categories of progress. Every freeze removed is one less support message, one less relaunch, and one more reason to stay in-session.

## What this means for players and server owners

For players, this update makes OPEN//77 easier to start using. Registration is smoother, sign-in is quicker, busy servers are easier to find, and the launcher now has a more connected identity layer through Discord.

For server owners, better onboarding and better discovery are both good news. The fewer steps between “I want to try CP2077 multiplayer” and “I joined a server,” the better the odds that curious players become regulars.

Today’s update is not about giant trailers or flashy systems. It is about removing friction from the platform and tightening up the experience around the edges. That is the kind of work that helps a **Cyberpunk 2077 dedicated server** ecosystem grow: faster entry, clearer discovery, stronger identity, and fewer technical interruptions.
