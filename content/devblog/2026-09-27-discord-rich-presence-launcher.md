OPEN//77 got a smaller but very visible quality-of-life upgrade today: the launcher now supports Discord Rich Presence for live OPEN//77 activity. For anyone following Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer progress, this is the kind of polish that helps the whole experience feel more connected before you even load into Night City.

## Discord Rich Presence comes to the launcher

The main change today is social rather than gameplay-focused. The OPEN//77 launcher can now show live activity through Discord Rich Presence, so Discord can reflect that you are active in the OPEN//77 ecosystem.

That matters more than it may sound at first. For a Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer mod, the time before and around play sessions is important too. Players coordinate in Discord, communities gather there, and RP groups often use status indicators as a quick way to see who is around. A launcher that can broadcast useful presence information helps OPEN//77 feel more like a real online platform instead of just a standalone executable.

## Why this matters for Cyberpunk 2077 online communities

A lot of multiplayer momentum comes from visibility. If friends can see that someone is active in OPEN//77, it becomes easier to message them, form a group, or jump into a session together. That is especially useful for the future of Cyberpunk 2077 co-op and for community-driven server play, where social friction can make the difference between a quiet night and a full crew heading into Night City.

This update also helps future Cyberpunk 2077 RP server communities. Roleplay groups usually organize through Discord first, then move into the game. Presence features are a small but practical part of that loop because they reduce guesswork and help players know when others are around.

## Stable launcher tracking updated

Alongside the Rich Presence feature, the backend has been updated to record the latest stable launcher release as version 110. That is not a flashy player-facing feature on its own, but it is the platform-side step that keeps the release state aligned with what players are actually using.

In plain terms: the new launcher state is recognized and tracked properly across the OPEN//77 platform.

## A small update that makes OPEN//77 feel bigger

Today was not about combat systems, syncing, or server gameplay. It was about presence, polish, and making the OPEN//77 platform feel alive outside the game window. Those touches matter for any Cyberpunk 2077 dedicated server ecosystem, because multiplayer is not only what happens in combat or in vehicles. It is also how players discover each other, organize sessions, and build habits around coming back.

As OPEN//77 keeps growing into a fuller Night City online experience, updates like this help connect the launcher, the community, and the game itself into one smoother flow.
