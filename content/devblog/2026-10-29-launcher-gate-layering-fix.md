Today’s OPEN//77 update is focused on the launcher experience. A new verified launcher build improves how gated access screens are layered, making the sign-in flow cleaner for players getting into our Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer mod.

## Cleaner launcher access flow

The main visible change today is in the OPEN//77 launcher. When an access gate is suspended, that gate is now pushed behind a transparent page instead of sitting in front in a confusing way.

That sounds small, but it fixes the kind of issue players notice immediately. Overlapping pages and half-active screens can make a launcher feel uncertain, especially during sign-in or when different access states are involved. This update makes the flow feel more intentional and easier to read.

## Why this matters for players

For players waiting to jump into Night City online, launcher friction is one of the worst kinds of friction. Before anyone sees gameplay, joins friends, or connects to a future Cyberpunk 2077 co-op session, the launcher has to do its job clearly.

This update helps remove a subtle source of confusion:

- fewer odd overlaps between launcher pages
- clearer visual priority during gated access
- less second-guessing about which screen is active

It is not a flashy feature drop, but it is exactly the kind of polish that makes the platform feel more solid.

## Verified release for the platform

Alongside the fix itself, the team has now reserved and verified launcher build 161 as the active release target.

For players, that means the platform is aligned on the same launcher version with the gate-layering fix included. For the team, that means fewer mismatched builds and a more predictable support baseline.

For future server owners, consistency matters too. A stable and verified launcher build reduces avoidable confusion when testing account flows, access restrictions, and onboarding for a Cyberpunk 2077 dedicated server environment.

## Small update, real usability win

Not every development day is about new combat systems, giant features, or world content. Some days are about removing rough edges. This was one of those days.

The result is a better first-contact experience for OPEN//77: cleaner launcher behavior, clearer gate handling, and one verified build everyone can rely on. That is good news for anyone following the project, whether you are here for a Cyberpunk 2077 RP server, broader Cyberpunk 2077 online play, or just curious about where OPEN//77 is heading next.

As always, the goal is simple: make getting into the game feel as smooth as playing it.
